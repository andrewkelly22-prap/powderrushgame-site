📅 A new Daily Challenge

One numbered mountain a day, the same for everyone, with three goals: collect snowflakes, jump a fallen tree, land a double back flip. Three tries, three stars and a big snowflake prize. Share your stars with friends.

👍 One-thumb skiing

Slide to carve, flick up to jump, flick down for speed. Back flips, front flips and spins off natural snow ramps.

🏔️ An endless mountain

Green to double black in a single run: glades, powder, moguls, rocky chutes and fallen trees, by day or by headlamp at night.

🎿 Your skier, your style

Collect snowflakes and dress up in the chalet: ski as a penguin, fox, bear, bunny or yeti, with jackets, hats and a crown, on skis or a snowboard.
