Security

Powder Rush and Happy Acorn Studio · last updated 30 October 2026

We take the security of our games seriously. Powder Rush collects no personal data and sends nothing from your device, which keeps the risk small, but if you find a security problem in the game or this website, we want to hear about it.

How to report a problem

Email development@powderrushgame.com with "Security" in the subject. Tell us what you found, how to reproduce it, and which version or page it affects. We'll reply within 3 business days and keep you updated until it's fixed. If you'd like, we'll thank you by name when the fix is released.

What's covered

  • The Powder Rush app for iPhone and iPad, and the game at powderrushgame.com/play
  • The powderrushgame.com website

Problems in Apple's, GitHub's or other companies' own services should go to them.

Please

  • Only test against your own device and your own copy of the game.
  • Don't access or change anyone else's data, and don't disrupt the website or the App Store.
  • No denial-of-service testing, social engineering or spam.
  • Give us a reasonable time to fix the problem before you talk about it publicly (we suggest 90 days).

If you follow these rules in good faith, we won't take legal action against you for your research. We don't offer payment for reports.

Fixes

Security fixes are free and arrive as normal App Store updates; keeping automatic updates on is the best protection. Once a security problem is fixed, we describe it here and in the update's notes.
