How to play

  • Carve: slide your thumb left or right (further for a harder turn); lift it to ski straight. On a computer, hold ← or →.
  • Jump: flick up (↑ or Space). Jump fallen trees, and jump for the floating ice-blue snowflakes.
  • Speed burst: flick down (↓) to push with your poles, or kick with your back foot on a snowboard.
  • Tricks in the air: flick sideways to spin, up for a back flip, down for a front flip. Finish a flip before you land! Tricks off a snow ramp score half as much again.
  • Outrun the avalanche: point downhill to pull away; slowing down lets it catch up.

Common questions

Where is my progress saved?

On your device only. Nothing is sent anywhere, so there's no account to sign in to. Deleting the game erases your progress, and so does Settings → Reset progress.

When does the daily challenge change?

At midnight where you are. Everyone gets the same challenge each day, and you can try as often as you like; your best try counts.

There's no sound.

Check Settings → Sound, and on an iPhone check the silent switch: the game stays quiet when your phone is on silent.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The app needs no internet at all.

Is it always night (or day)?

By default, day and night follow the real sun where you are. Settings → Time of day lets you choose Day or Night instead.

Are there ads or purchases?

No ads and no purchases. Snowflakes are earned by skiing and spent in the chalet on kit for your skier.
